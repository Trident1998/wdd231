NP Membership Benefits

  • No membership fee for registered non-profit organizations.
  • Free directory listing on the chamber website.
  • Access to all chamber networking events.
  • Inclusion in the chamber newsletter.
  • Eligibility for non-profit collaboration programs.

Bronze Membership Benefits

  • All NP-level benefits.
  • Listing in the printed chamber directory.
  • 10% discount on chamber events and workshops.
  • Rotation in the monthly newsletter sponsor spotlight.
  • Early access to event registration.

Silver Membership Benefits

  • All Bronze-level benefits.
  • Featured listing on the chamber directory page.
  • Quarterly training and professional development sessions.
  • 20% discount on chamber events.
  • Logo placement in the chamber newsletter.
  • Invitations to private member roundtables.

Gold Membership Benefits

  • All Silver-level benefits.
  • Spotlight position rotation on the chamber home page.
  • Free event sponsorship rotation each year.
  • Dedicated chamber account manager.
  • 30% discount on all chamber programs.
  • Priority access to investment forums and trade missions.
